Utre

Stop planning. Do it. (утре means “tomorrow”)

Utre is a personal task manager for Android, Linux and macOS. Your tasks live on your own devices, sync directly between them, and show up on a calendar next to your Google Calendar events.

What it does

  • Local-first: tasks, lists and nested tags are stored on your devices. There is no Utre server and no account.
  • Peer-to-peer sync: your phone and computers sync directly with each other.
  • Calendar: day, week and month views with drag-to-schedule.
  • Google Calendar (optional): see your Google events read-only, and show your scheduled tasks on a separate calendar that Utre creates.

Google data

If you sign in with Google, Utre reads your calendar events and manages only its own calendar. The data stays on your devices and is never sent to the developer. Details are in the privacy policy.
