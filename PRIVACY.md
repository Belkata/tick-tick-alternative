# Utre privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-25

Utre is a personal, local-first task manager. It has no server and no accounts of its own.
This page explains what happens to your data, including data from your Google account.

## What Utre stores, and where
- Your tasks, lists, tags and settings are stored in a database **on your own devices**.
- If you pair two devices, they sync **directly with each other** (peer to peer, encrypted). The
  data does not pass through a server run by the developer.
- The developer receives no data from the app: there is no analytics, no tracking and no
  advertising.

## Google Calendar (optional)
If you sign in with Google, Utre asks for these permissions:
- **Read your calendars and events** (`calendar.readonly`), to show your Google events in
  Utre's calendar, read-only.
- **Create and manage only the calendar Utre creates** (`calendar.app.created`), to show your
  scheduled tasks on Google Calendar.

How that data is handled:
- Events are downloaded to the device that fetched them and kept in its local database. They
  are **not** sent to other devices by Utre's sync, and not sent to the developer or anyone else.
- Task titles, notes and times are sent **to Google** only to create events in the calendar
  Utre manages.
- Sign-in tokens are stored on the device, in the operating system's secure storage. They are
  not synced between devices.
- Utre uses Google data only to provide these features. It does not sell it, share it, use it
  for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Utre's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your control
- **Sign out** in Utre's settings to delete the cached Google events and the stored tokens from
  that device.
- **Revoke access** at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
- **Delete everything** by uninstalling the app or deleting its database file.
